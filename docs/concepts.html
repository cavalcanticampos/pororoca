Concepts

TermMeaning
MalocaA team, declared in maloca.yaml. Its mode is solo (one human, and the fleet works for them) or team (several humans, each with their own lane). The word is the Amazonian communal house.
AgentAn autonomous Claude Code session with a role, a schedule and tools. It is not a script that returns JSON.
CardOne unit of work: a request, an alert or an issue. Its states follow OpenRig's queue: pending, in-progress, blocked, done, failed, denied, canceled.
Lane "You"The cards that truly need the human, and only those.
GatekeeperThe only agent allowed to put a card in a human's lane.
Autonomy levelFor each kind of action, whether the fleet may act alone, needs the judge, or needs the human. Every level starts at propose only.
JudgeThe agent that reviews sensitive actions, such as CI config, migrations, infrastructure and secrets.
ChaserThe agent that owns follow-up. A card waiting on someone always has one.
Shadow modeThe fleet proposes and sends nothing. It is how a new fleet earns trust and produces its first metric.
JirauThe cockpit: a Claude Code pane next to your chat with your priorities and your lane. The word is the raised wooden platform of riverside houses.
