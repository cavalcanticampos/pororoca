Tour

Four scenes from the reference fleet's daily work. Names are made up.

1. An issue becomes a merged PR

A bug report lands in the tracker. Triage classifies it, the investigator finds the cause and writes it up, the builder opens a PR with a test, and the judge reviews it against the autonomy rules. The deliverer watches CI and merges. You see one line in the cockpit: fixed, merged, in production.

2. A chat question answered with your OK

A colleague asks in chat whether a feature flag is on in production. The fleet checks and drafts the reply; at the starting level, propose, the draft waits in your lane and goes out when you run send N. pororoca metrics counts how many drafts you approved unchanged. That number is what earns a level auto.

3. The chaser on a card that is waiting

A fix needs a review from someone outside the fleet. The card goes to waiting on Ana, not to done. The chaser nudges once a working day, escalates after two days with no movement and closes the card only when the work is really done.

4. Your lane

Out of a hundred things that arrive, about seven need you. The gatekeeper is the only agent that can put a card in your lane. Each card comes with one line on what is missing, a proposed answer and a link. You answer in your own words, and the fleet does the rest.
