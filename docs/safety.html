Safety

An agent that speaks for you is the most sensitive thing Pororoca does. These defaults are not optional.

Shadow mode firstA new fleet proposes and sends nothing until you have seen what it would have done.
Autonomy is earnedEach kind of action starts at propose only, and you raise it one level at a time, with numbers.
Always signedEvery message sent on your behalf ends with 🤖.
A judge for sensitive changesCI config, migrations, infrastructure and secrets always go to the judge or to you.
Secrets by referenceNever in plain text in chat, cards or PRs.
Chasing people is offuntil you turn it on.

The embarrassment metric

This is the share of messages sent on your behalf that you later edited or retracted. Pororoca tracks it from day one and aims to keep it under 1%.

Terms of service

Acting as a user on a chat platform can conflict with its terms. Each adapter documents this. A bot identity is always available as the alternative.
