Pororoca and OpenRig

They answer two different questions. OpenRig asks how a team of coding agents can live and work together on your machine. Pororoca asks how one busy person gets off the critical path. Pororoca is built to be compatible with OpenRig, not to replace it.

OpenRigPororoca
Centre of gravityThe agent team: seats, pods, queue, continuityThe human: the gatekeeper, their lane, the share resolved without them
Team fileRigSpecmaloca.yaml, a RigSpec plus x-maloca, exportable
Speaks for the humanNo; its Slack app asks the operator, as a botYes, within declared autonomy levels and signed 🤖, or as a bot
Follow-upWakes and escalates agentsAlso chases people outside the fleet until the work closes
ModesOne operator over a teamsolo or team
MaturityReleased, with a large integration matrixPre-alpha

If you want a long-lived team of coding agents, start with OpenRig. If what drowns you is everything that lands on you, that is Pororoca's job, and the two can run together.
