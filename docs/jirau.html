Jirau

The cockpit. A pane beside your Claude Code chat with the two things that matter: what needs you, and what the fleet is doing. A jirau is the raised wooden platform in a riverside house, where you keep what must stay out of the water.

Install

# copy the pane into Claude Code (~/.claude/skills/pororoca-jirau)
pororoca jirau install
# open Claude Code where your maloca.yaml lives: the pane opens beside the chat
claude

The pane opens by itself only in a folder that holds a fleet, so it stays quiet in every other session. Anywhere else, /jirau opens it; set POROROCA_DIR to point it at a fleet and POROROCA_BIN if pororoca is not on your PATH. pororoca jirau uninstall removes it.

What it shows

The fleetRunning or stopped, paused by a usage limit, and how many cards sit in the repair queue.
Your laneOnly the cards the gatekeeper sent you, plus waits the chaser escalated. Open one to read the reason, the draft and the PR.
What the fleet is onEvery open card with its real state: triaging, investigating, building, fixing red CI, waiting on a person or a PR. Never "delivered" while it is still open; stalled cards get a ⚠.
The metricShare of work resolved without you, and your corrections this week.

Acting from the pane

Open a card and press send, merge, later or ignore. For anything that needs your words, type it:

/jirau send 3
/jirau edit 3 Friday at 15:00 works better
/jirau decide 4 keep the export job until March
/jirau merge 7
/jirau ignore 9

Every action runs the same CLI (pororoca send|edit|decide|merge|ignore|later N), so the pane never holds state of its own. It reads pororoca jirau snapshot --json every 10 seconds; r refreshes now, and a toast tells you when a new card needs you.
