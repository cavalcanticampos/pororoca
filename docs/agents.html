Agents

Every agent is an autonomous Claude Code session with one plain job. The names come from Amazonian legends, because a name you remember is easier to talk about than "agent 4".

The starter maloca

pororoca init writes these six. Together they cover the whole path from "something arrived" to "it is really done".

Guaraná

gatekeeper

The only agent that can put a card in your lane. Everything else it sends back to the fleet with a reason.

In a Sateré-Mawé story, the eyes of a beloved child were planted and grew into the guaraná fruit, which still looks like an open eye.

Curupira

triage

Reads everything that arrives, decides what kind of work it is and sends it down the right path.

A forest guardian with flaming hair and feet turned backwards, who sends anyone who wanders in down the wrong trail.

Boto

investigator

Finds the real cause, checks whether someone already fixed it and writes up what the builder needs.

The pink river dolphin who walks into village parties as a stranger and quietly learns everyone's secrets.

Boitatá

builder

Writes the fix with a test, opens the PR and keeps it green until it is ready to merge.

A serpent made of fire that glides over the fields at night and protects them from anyone who would burn them.

Uirapuru

deliverer

Watches CI, merges within your autonomy rules, follows the change to production and tells whoever asked.

A small forest bird whose song is so rare that the whole forest goes quiet to hear it.

Iara

chaser

Owns every card that waits on someone. Nudges people and PRs on schedule until the work is really done.

The lady of the river, whose voice calls from the water and is impossible to ignore.

Optional agents

Turn these on in maloca.yaml when you need them. Each one came from a gap the reference fleet hit in practice.

Muiraquitã

judge

Reviews sensitive changes (CI config, migrations, infra, secrets) against your rules, and escalates only what it should not decide.

A frog carved in green stone, given as a pledge of good fortune.

Caipora

CI sentinel

Watches the default branches. A red build becomes a card on its own, grouped so one failure is one card.

A small guardian of the forest animals who keeps watch at night and punishes careless hunters.

Vitória-régia

skill smith

Spots a procedure that shows up in three or more reports and turns it into a reusable skill for the fleet.

A girl who longed to touch the moon dove into the river and became the giant water lily that floats under it.

Matinta

video proof

After a change lands in staging, records the screen doing the thing, so 'done' comes with a video.

A night bird whose whistle announces a visit, and who comes back the next morning to collect a promise.

Names are labels, not personalities. Rename any agent in x-maloca.agents; the role is what the core reads.
